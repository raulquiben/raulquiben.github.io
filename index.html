Profile

Aerospace engineer completing a PhD (defence expected December 2026) on the design and trajectory optimization of hydrogen-powered aircraft at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. I turn open engineering questions into well-posed optimization problems and solve them with my own Python tools: multidisciplinary design optimization, optimal control (direct and indirect methods) and large-scale data analysis of flights, weather and contrails. My work is peer-reviewed and won a national innovation award, and I have researched in Spain, Italy, the US (Stanford) and Japan (Tohoku).

What I work on

MDO

Aircraft design optimization

Python/OpenMDAO platforms (MOTIVATION) coupling aerodynamics, weights, LH₂ tanks, propulsion, mission and cost models to size hydrogen and hybrid-electric aircraft for minimum emissions and cost.

PERF · MISSION

Aircraft performance

Point-mass flight dynamics and segment-by-segment mission analysis across the flight envelope: drag polars calibrated against BADA, Kriging surrogates of engine decks, take-off to landing fuel burn and payload-range trade-offs for conventional and hydrogen aircraft.

OCP · 4D

Trajectory optimization

Optimal-cruise solutions with Pontryagin's Maximum Principle and 3D/4D direct-method flight planning over real weather fields, minimising fuel, time and climate impact.

DATA · CO₂ · non-CO₂

Flight, weather & contrail data

Python pipelines that join ADS-B / OpenSky flight tracks, ERA5 reanalysis and ground-camera images; contrail modelling (CoCiP, pycontrails, climate-change functions) and LES validation at Stanford.
